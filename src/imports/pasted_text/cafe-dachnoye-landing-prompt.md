```
↓

— Feeling: "My place, I know it's good here"
↓

```

## UX Principles Applied:

1. **Jakob's Law** — Users expect familiar patterns. Navigation and structure must be predictable.

2. **Hick's Law** — Decision time increases with options. Menu must be structured, choice simple.

3. **Fitts's Law** — Time to reach target depends on distance and size. CTA buttons — large, visible.

4. **Miller's Law** — People hold 7±2 items in memory. Benefits — max 6. Dishes — max 8 per screen.

5. **Aesthetic Usability Effect** — Users perceive beautiful interfaces as more usable.

6. **Gestalt Proximity** — Nearby elements perceived as a group.

7. **Social Proof** — Reviews, ratings, visitor count build trust.

8. **Anchoring** — First price sets anchor. 200 ₽ for lunch — excellent anchor.

9. **Peak-End Rule** — Users remember emotionally intense moments and the end. Gallery and map must be strong.
